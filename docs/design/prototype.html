WinIsland prototype

Interactive mock with fake data. It shows the look and the motion only. Spotify, Windows notifications and games get tested in Claude Code. Hotkeys work here too: Ctrl+Alt+I to expand, Ctrl+Alt+H to hide, Alt+X to hide notifications. You can also click the island to expand it and swipe a notification sideways to hide it.

Music

Click the island or press Ctrl+Alt+I to expand it, then use play, previous and next. With no music, expanding does nothing. Click the open island outside the buttons to close it, or wait 2 seconds after the pointer leaves.

Notifications

Tap a notification to see the last 3, newest first. Swipe it sideways to hide one, or hide all. The banner vanishes after 6 seconds.
