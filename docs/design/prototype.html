WinIsland prototype

Interactive mock with fake data, v1.3. It shows the look and the motion only. New in v1.3: the page dots show on opening and on a page change and fade after 1.5 seconds, a white dot next to the clock for notifications you have not seen on the notifications page, Discord's logo on a blurple circle, the island size choice, and three app icon concepts at the bottom. New in v1.4 (draft): messages from the same chat stack into one card ("Same chat again", "Four from one chat"), and a Settings switch that also clears hidden notifications from Windows. "Look: Glass" shows the glass island; "Game scene: Bright" puts a bright game behind it. New in v1.5 (draft): a speaker button right of next that swaps the progress bar for the music app's volume (or Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down while open), "YouTube in Chrome" with wide artwork, Claude and Snipping Tool notifications with their own logo in white on their colour, and camera and microphone dots under "Privacy (v1.5)". Spotify, Windows notifications and games get tested in Claude Code. Click the island or press Ctrl+Alt+I to open it, even when it shows the clock. On the open island, scroll down or press Alt+Down for notifications, scroll up or Alt+Up for music, or click the dots on the right. Ctrl+Alt+H hides the island, Alt+X hides notifications. Swipe a notification banner sideways to hide it.

Music

Paused: the compact island shows the paused song for 5 seconds, then the clock. Click the clock to get the paused song back and press play. With Spotify closed, opening the island shows the empty view. The four sample covers are red, blue, very light and black and white; their colours are worked out from the cover pixels, as the app will.

Notifications

Tap a banner to open the notifications page. Clicking a card does nothing. A message that arrives while the notifications page is open goes straight to the top of the page, without a banner. v1.4: messages from the same chat stack into one card that says how many there are; click a stack to see its last 3 messages, click again or press Alt+Left to go back.

Privacy (v1.5)

An orange dot while any app uses the microphone, a green one while any app uses the camera; with both, green. They show on the clock and the music pill, at the right end. The camera dot is untested in the app (no webcam).

Calls (v1.4)

WhatsApp Web only. The call banner stays while the call rings and has no buttons; clicking it does nothing. Alt+X hides it on the island only (in the app a swipe does too). A missed call arrives as an ordinary WhatsApp notification.
