WinIsland prototype

Interactive mock with fake data, v1.1. It shows the look and the motion only. Spotify, Windows notifications and games get tested in Claude Code. Click the island or press Ctrl+Alt+I to open it, even when it shows the clock. On the open island, scroll down or press Alt+Down for notifications, scroll up or Alt+Up for music, or click the dots on the right. Ctrl+Alt+H hides the island, Alt+X hides notifications. Swipe a notification banner sideways to hide it.

Music

Paused: the compact island shows the paused song for 5 seconds, then the clock. Click the clock to get the paused song back and press play. With Spotify closed, opening the island shows the empty view. The four sample covers are red, blue, very light and black and white; their colours are worked out from the cover pixels, as the app will.

Notifications

Tap a banner to open the notifications page. Clicking a card does nothing. A message that arrives while the notifications page is open goes straight to the top of the page, without a banner.
